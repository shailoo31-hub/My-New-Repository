😄 The joke is really about how someone creates the problem, then becomes the victim of the problem:</br>
Five flatmates—A, B, C, D and E—live together.<br>
One day, the sugar finishes.

Everyone says to the ration guy, “Please buy sugar.”

He confidently says, “Sure, I'll get it.”

A little later, he comes back and announces:

**“Done! I bought the sugar and kept it in the kitchen.”**

Everyone: “Great!”

The next morning, the cook asks, “Where is the sugar?”

Everyone: “It's in the kitchen.”

They search.

Nothing.

They search again.

Still nothing.

Everyone searches together.

**No sugar.**

So they call the ration guy.

“Where is the sugar? You said you bought it!”

He immediately disconnects the call.

Then he turns to the friends sitting with him in the park and says:

**“You know what? These flatmates are terrible! They are ALWAYS angry with me. Even today, they're blaming me for not buying sugar!”**

His friend looks at him and asks:

**“But… did you buy the sugar?”**

He says:

**“No.”**

Friend: “Then why are you angry with them?”

He pauses for a moment and says:

**“Because they found out.”** 😂
